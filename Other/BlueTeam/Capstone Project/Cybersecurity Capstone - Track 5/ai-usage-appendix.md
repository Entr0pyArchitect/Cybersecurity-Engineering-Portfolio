# AI Usage Appendix

ChatGPT was used as a drafting and planning assistant during this project. Its role included helping organize documentation, improving the wording of project descriptions, and assisting with the structure of helper scripts used in the lab environment.

All AI-generated material was reviewed, edited, and validated before being included in the project. Environment details, tool outputs, file structure, commands, and results were verified directly by the student inside the lab system.

No real credentials, sensitive personal data, third-party data, or unauthorized target information were provided to the AI. The project remains limited to synthetic data and a personal offline lab environment.

AI assistance was used to support clarity and organization, but final responsibility for accuracy, testing, scope, and submission content remains with the student.
